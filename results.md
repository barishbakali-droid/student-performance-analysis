# 📊 Student Performance Analysis Results

## 📌 Dataset Summary

The dataset contains performance information for **20 students**, including study hours, attendance, previous scores, and final scores.

## 📈 Key Statistics

| Metric | Average |
|---|---:|
| Study Hours | 3.90 |
| Attendance | 85.15% |
| Previous Score | 75.25 |
| Final Score | 80.15 |

## 🔍 Key Findings

- 📚 Average study time: **3.9 hours**
- 📅 Average attendance: **85.15%**
- 📝 Average previous score: **75.25**
- 🎯 Average final score: **80.15**
- 📈 Students with higher study hours generally achieved higher final scores.
- 📊 Higher attendance was also associated with better final performance.
